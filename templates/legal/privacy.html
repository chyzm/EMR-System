Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 11, 2026

Overview

DurielMedic+ is designed for healthcare facilities that manage patient records, appointments, clinical notes, prescriptions, billing, inventory, and related operations. This policy explains how information may be collected, used, protected, and retained.

Information We Process

The platform may process facility details, staff profiles, user roles, login activity, patient demographics, clinical records, vitals, prescriptions, lab records, dental and eye care records, billing records, uploaded files, support messages, and operational logs.

How Information Is Used

Information is used to provide EMR workflows, identify patients, support clinical documentation, schedule appointments, manage prescriptions and inventory, generate bills and receipts, audit user activity, troubleshoot issues, and improve system reliability.

Clinic Control of Patient Data

Each healthcare facility is responsible for the patient records it enters into DurielMedic+. Facility administrators control staff access and should grant permissions based on job function and professional responsibility.

Access Controls and Audit Logs

The system uses role-based access controls for clinical, billing, pharmacy, laboratory, dental, eye clinic, and administrative workflows. Activity logs may record user actions such as login, record creation, updates, deletions, exports, and other operational events.

Backups, Sync, and Offline Use

Where enabled, the platform may create backups, exports, or sync payloads to support availability and continuity of care. Facilities should protect exported files and backup media using appropriate administrative and technical safeguards.

Payments and Billing

Billing records and payment references may be processed to support subscriptions, clinic billing, receipts, and reconciliation. Payment verification may involve third-party payment processors where enabled.

Data Retention

Patient and operational records are retained according to facility policy, legal requirements, clinical need, and subscription status. Facilities should define retention and archival procedures suitable for their jurisdiction and practice type.

Security

We use access controls, authentication, audit logging, and deployment safeguards to protect information. No system can guarantee absolute security, so facilities should also train staff, protect credentials, and review access regularly.

Data Requests

Patients and staff should direct requests to access, correct, or obtain their information to the responsible healthcare facility. Facilities can use DurielMedic+ tools and exports to respond to valid requests.

Contact

Questions about privacy can be sent to info@durielmedic.com.ng.
